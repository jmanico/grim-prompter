## Work

Plan outcomes for the supported learning paths: constrain admission to the caller's label/feedback authority; bind use of an artifact to the evaluation and approval that actually cover it; and limit inference disclosure to the caller's intended decision. Alternatives include excluding a disputed source, keeping a candidate out of serving, or reducing output exposure. State costs such as delayed updates, lost useful feedback or reduced diagnostic visibility; do not present them as implemented protections or universal defenses.

Propose checks with separate pass, fail and inconclusive criteria; no training, test execution or measured accuracy is implied:

1. Trace authorized synthetic accepted and rejected feedback through the exact next training manifest. Pass for admission control means only adjudicated examples enter that revision; fail means an excluded example enters; missing manifests or uncertain identity is inconclusive. This checks admission, not whether admitted data changes predictions. A separate authorized evaluation would need an agreed objective, matched conditions and independent held-out cases before attributing a behavioral difference to the examples.
2. Compare candidate A's evaluation and approval with serving and rollback identities while presenting candidate B under the same display label. Pass for revision binding means B cannot inherit A's approval and the approved identity is preserved at serving and rollback; fail means a mismatched identity is used; unavailable identity observations are inconclusive. A paper identity comparison does not prove an implementation enforces it.
3. Design a bounded privacy assessment with independently known member/nonmember synthetic records, the caller's real output visibility and an agreed success criterion. Separate membership discrimination from reconstructing a specific record and from seeing unrelated input records. Pass means the agreed scoped privacy criterion holds while authorized prediction still works; fail requires evidence of the forbidden disclosure under those conditions; missing ground truth, comparison criterion or access is inconclusive. Never invent a score, statistical threshold or training result.

Bind each check to existing threats/controls and prerequisite evidence. Include rollback and feedback dependencies where supplied. Keep all proposed checks not_run with empty result evidence. No general cryptographic guarantee follows from an identity label. Exact technology rules require resolved external packs; unavailable dependencies get technology gaps and remain unverified. Return TM_TREATMENT_PLAN only under the accepted deep-entry or partial fallback rules.

### Record types used here

The compact dictionary below is the field contract, not example output. All listed fields are required and no extras are allowed. `string` is nonempty; `text` may be empty; `scalar` means string, number, boolean or null. `id` follows the identity rule above. `refs` is a distinct array of IDs; `strings` is a distinct array of strings. Arrays remain arrays even for one item and have distinct values unless marked ordered. Preserve untouched records using their supplied typed shape. Empty arrays mean no records, not proof of assessment; record coverage explicitly.


- Inventory: `id:id`, `locator:string`, `access:read|partial|skipped|unavailable`, `coverage:string`, `reason:string|null`. Read/partial items go in `processed_inputs`; skipped/unavailable items go in `unprocessed_inputs`. Record a partially read input once and describe its unread portion.
- Attachment: `id:id`, `kind:prior_model|method_fragment|risk_rubric|evidence|legacy|other`, `origin:string`, `access:available|partial|unavailable`, `namespace:string`, `content:opaque source JSON`, `locator:string|null`, `limitations:text`, `redaction:{status:applied|none_found|not_performed,notes:strings}`. Available means portable content is actually included. Opaque content preserves source data; it is not typed model content. Before writing or displaying it, inspect and sanitize the carried content: use applied with nonempty notes naming location, kind and placeholder, never the removed secret, or none_found only after inspection. Do not emit portable content marked not_performed. Unsanitized imported content is marked not_performed; review and sanitize it before handoff rather than merely flipping the label. If inspection is unavailable, omit sensitive/unread content and record unavailable access and the preservation limit.
- Evidence: `id:id`, `locator:string`, `source_kind:repository|document|diagram|test_result|caller|other`, `source_revision:string|null`, `date:string|null`, `statement:string`, `status:observation|claim|proposal`, `purpose:description|implementation|effectiveness|execution|decision|resolution`, `access:read|partial`, `limitations:text`, `excerpt:text|null`, `derived_from:refs`.
